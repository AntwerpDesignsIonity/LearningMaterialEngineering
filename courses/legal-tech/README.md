# Legal Technology & IoT

## Course Overview
Explore the intersection of law, legal technology, and IoT systems. Learn about regulatory compliance, privacy laws, smart contracts, and legal applications of IoT in the legal profession.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand IoT regulations and compliance (GDPR, CCPA, etc.)
- Implement privacy-preserving IoT systems
- Learn smart contracts and blockchain for IoT
- Apply IoT in legal practice and law enforcement
- Navigate intellectual property in IoT
