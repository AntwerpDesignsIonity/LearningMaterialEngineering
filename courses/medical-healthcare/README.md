# Medical & Healthcare IoT

## Course Overview
Comprehensive training on Internet of Medical Things (IoMT) covering medical devices, remote patient monitoring, hospital IoT systems, and healthcare data analytics.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand medical IoT devices and systems
- Implement remote patient monitoring solutions
- Ensure HIPAA compliance and patient privacy
- Deploy hospital IoT infrastructure
- Analyze healthcare data for insights

## Course Structure

### Module 1: Healthcare IoT Fundamentals
- Introduction to IoMT (Internet of Medical Things)
- Healthcare Industry Challenges
- Regulatory Landscape (FDA, HIPAA, MDR)
- Clinical Workflow Integration

### Module 2: Medical Devices and Sensors
- Wearable Health Monitors
- Implantable Medical Devices
- Diagnostic Equipment Connectivity
- Biosensors and Vital Sign Monitoring

### Module 3: Remote Patient Monitoring
- RPM System Architecture
- Chronic Disease Management

- Elderly Care Solutions

## Medical Device Categories
- Class I: Low risk (thermometers, stethoscopes)
- Class II: Moderate risk (infusion pumps, ventilators)
- Class III: High risk (pacemakers, implants)

## Key Technologies

- Zigbee Health
- Medical-Grade WiFi
- 5G in Healthcare
