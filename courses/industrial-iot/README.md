# Industrial IoT (IIoT)

## Course Overview
Master Industrial Internet of Things technologies for manufacturing, logistics, and industrial applications with focus on reliability, scalability, and real-time operations.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand IIoT architecture and standards
- Implement industrial sensor networks
- Deploy SCADA and MES integration
- Build predictive maintenance systems
- Ensure industrial-grade security

## Course Structure

### Module 1: IIoT Fundamentals
- Industry 4.0 Concepts
- IIoT vs Consumer IoT
- Industrial Protocols (OPC UA, Modbus, PROFINET)
- Digital Twin Technology

### Module 2: Industrial Sensors and Devices
- Vibration Sensors
- Pressure and Flow Sensors
- Industrial PLCs
- Edge Gateways

### Module 3: Industrial Networks
- Fieldbus Systems
- TSN (Time-Sensitive Networking)
- 5G for Industry
- Network Redundancy

### Module 4: Data Acquisition Systems
- SCADA Integration
- Historian Databases
- Real-Time Data Processing
- Data Quality and Validation

### Module 5: Predictive Maintenance
- Condition Monitoring
- Failure Prediction Models
- Maintenance Scheduling
- ROI Analysis

### Module 6: Asset Management
- Asset Tracking Systems
- Inventory Management
- Lifecycle Management
- Digital Asset Records

### Module 7: Quality Control
- Automated Inspection
- Computer Vision in Manufacturing
- Statistical Process Control
- Defect Detection

### Module 8: Supply Chain IoT
- Logistics Tracking
- Warehouse Automation
- Fleet Management
- Cold Chain Monitoring

### Module 9: Energy Management
- Industrial Energy Monitoring
- Power Quality Analysis
- Efficiency Optimization
- Carbon Footprint Tracking

### Module 10: Safety Systems
- Industrial Safety Sensors
- Emergency Response Systems
- Worker Safety Monitoring
- Compliance Tracking

## Industry Standards
- ISA-95 (Manufacturing Operations)
- OPC UA
- MQTT Sparkplug
- ISO/IEC 62443 (Security)

## Prerequisites
- IoT Fundamentals
- Industrial automation knowledge
- Networking concepts
- Programming skills
