# IoT & AI Training Course: Full-Scale Home and Industrial Sync

Welcome to the comprehensive IoT and AI training platform! This repository contains a complete curriculum for learning Internet of Things (IoT) and Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies with applications across multiple professional fields.

## 🎯 Course Overview

This full-scale training program bridges the gap between IoT technology and practical applications in diverse fields including:

- **Home Automation** - Smart home systems and residential IoT
- **Industrial IoT** - Manufacturing, logistics, and Industry 4.0
- **Legal Technology** - Compliance, privacy, and legal applications
- **Medical & Healthcare** - IoMT, remote patient monitoring, and hospital systems
- **Aerospace & Defense** - Aviation systems, military IoT, and unmanned vehicles
- **AI Integration** - Machine learning and intelligent automation across all domains

## 📚 Course Structure

### 1. [IoT Fundamentals](courses/iot-fundamentals/)
Learn the core concepts, architectures, and technologies that power IoT systems.
- **Duration:** 8 weeks (40 hours)
- **Topics:** Hardware, protocols, platforms, security
- **Prerequisites:** Basic programming knowledge

### 2. [AI & IoT Integration](courses/ai-iot-integration/)
Master the convergence of AI and IoT for intelligent, adaptive systems.
- **Duration:** 10 weeks (50 hours)
- **Topics:** TinyML, edge AI, computer vision, predictive analytics
- **Prerequisites:** IoT Fundamentals, Python

### 3. [Home Automation](courses/home-automation/)
Build complete smart home systems with modern IoT technologies.
- **Duration:** 8 weeks (40 hours)
- **Topics:** Smart lighting, climate control, security, voice assistants
- **Prerequisites:** IoT Fundamentals

### 4. [Industrial IoT](courses/industrial-iot/)
Implement Industry 4.0 solutions for manufacturing and industrial applications.
- **Duration:** 12 weeks (60 hours)
- **Topics:** SCADA, predictive maintenance, asset tracking, supply chain
- **Prerequisites:** IoT Fundamentals, industrial knowledge

### 5. [Legal Technology & IoT](courses/legal-tech/)
Navigate regulations, compliance, and legal applications of IoT.
- **Duration:** 8 weeks (40 hours)
- **Topics:** GDPR, smart contracts, e-discovery, privacy
- **Prerequisites:** Basic legal knowledge, IoT Fundamentals

### 6. [Medical & Healthcare IoT](courses/medical-healthcare/)
Deploy IoMT solutions for healthcare delivery and patient monitoring.
- **Duration:** 12 weeks (60 hours)
- **Topics:** Remote patient monitoring, HIPAA compliance, telemedicine
- **Prerequisites:** IoT Fundamentals, healthcare terminology

### 7. [Aerospace & Defense](courses/aerospace-defense/)
Implement IoT in aviation, military, and defense applications.
- **Duration:** 14 weeks (70 hours)
- **Topics:** Aircraft monitoring, UAS, tactical networks, military-grade security
- **Prerequisites:** IoT Fundamentals, systems engineering

### 8. [Cross-Field Applications](courses/cross-field-applications/)
Integrate IoT solutions across multiple industries and domains.
- **Duration:** 10 weeks (50 hours)
- **Topics:** Multi-domain architecture, interoperability, complex integrations
- **Prerequisites:** At least 2 sector-specific courses

IoT Fundamentals → Industrial IoT → AI & IoT Integration → Cross-Field Applications

IoT Fundamentals → Medical & Healthcare → Legal Tech → AI & IoT Integration

## 🛠️ Technologies Covered

- **Hardware:** Raspberry Pi, ESP32, Arduino, Industrial PLCs
- **Protocols:** MQTT, CoAP, HTTP/HTTPS, OPC UA, LoRaWAN, Zigbee
- **Platforms:** AWS IoT, Azure IoT Hub, Google Cloud IoT
- **AI/ML:** TensorFlow Lite, PyTorch, Edge Impulse
- **Languages:** Python, C/C++, JavaScript, Node.js
- **Standards:** IEEE, ISO, HL7, HIPAA, GDPR
