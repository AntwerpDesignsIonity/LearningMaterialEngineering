# IoT & AI Training Platform - Project Summary

## Overview

This repository contains a comprehensive, full-scale training curriculum for Internet of Things (IoT) and Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies with applications spanning multiple professional fields.

## What Was Built

### 🎓 Complete Course Structure (8 Major Courses)

1. **IoT Fundamentals** (IOT-101)
   - 8 weeks, 40 hours
   - Foundation course covering hardware, protocols, security, and cloud platforms
   - Includes lessons, labs, and assessments

2. **AI & IoT Integration** (AI-IOT-201)
   - 10 weeks, 50 hours
   - TinyML, edge AI, computer vision, predictive analytics
   - Advanced machine learning for IoT systems

3. **Home Automation** (HOME-201)
   - 8 weeks, 40 hours
   - Smart home systems, Home Assistant, voice control
   - Practical home IoT implementations

4. **Industrial IoT** (IIOT-301)
   - 12 weeks, 60 hours
   - Industry 4.0, SCADA, predictive maintenance
   - Manufacturing and industrial applications

5. **Legal Technology & IoT** (LEGAL-201)
   - 8 weeks, 40 hours
   - GDPR compliance, smart contracts, e-discovery
   - Legal applications and regulations

6. **Medical & Healthcare IoT** (MED-301)
   - 12 weeks, 60 hours
   - Remote patient monitoring, HIPAA compliance, telemedicine
   - Healthcare IoT systems and applications

7. **Aerospace & Defense IoT** (AERO-401)
   - 14 weeks, 70 hours
   - Aircraft monitoring, UAS systems, military applications
   - Aviation and defense IoT

8. **Cross-Field Applications** (CROSS-401)
   - 10 weeks, 50 hours
   - Multi-domain integration, interoperability
   - Complex cross-industry IoT solutions
